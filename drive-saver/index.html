Drive Saver

A Telegram bot by BotVerse that saves files you send it into your own Google Drive.

How it works

  1. Open Drive Saver in Telegram and tap Connect Google Drive.
  2. Sign in with Google and allow access. Drive Saver asks only for the drive.file permission: it can see and change only the files it creates for you, and nothing else in your Drive.
  3. Send or forward a file to the bot. It is uploaded into a "Telegram Saver" folder in your Drive and you get a link to it.

Your control

  • /disconnect removes the bot's access to your Google account.
  • /deletemydata removes access and deletes everything the bot keeps about you.
  • You can also remove access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
